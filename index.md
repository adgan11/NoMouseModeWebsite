# No Mouse Mode: your keyboard is the mouse

Source: https://nomousemode.com/  
Mac menu bar app · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple silicon and Intel · $19.99 one-time for 1 Mac · free updates

No Mouse Mode lets you use your Mac without reaching for the mouse or trackpad. Click any button by typing the two letters on it, or just its name. Glide the pointer, drag, scroll and jump across screens with the keys under your fingers.

## Click anything by typing

- **Click labels (⇧⌘Space):** works in any app, even with No Mouse Mode off. Every button, link, tab, menu, menu bar item and Dock icon gets a small label; type it and it's clicked. Hold ⇧ with the last letter to right-click, ⌘ to ⌘-click (new tab), ⌥ to double-click, ⌃ to just move the pointer there. Tab keeps the labels coming back for several clicks. Labels for a window stay the same each time.
- **Type to click (⇧⌘/):** type part of a button's or text's name (case, spaces and accents ignored; initials work) and press Return.
- **Labels in every app (optional):** for apps that don't describe their buttons to macOS, No Mouse Mode can read on-screen text with Apple's on-device text recognition. Needs Screen Recording; nothing is saved or sent.

Every key and shortcut can be changed in Settings. Shortcuts with ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥ always reach the app you're using, and clicking into a text box turns No Mouse Mode off so you can type. You can also list apps where it stays off, or hold Right Command or Right Option to use it only while the key is down.

## Scroll areas and the jump grid

- **Scroll areas (⇧⌘J):** numbers every scrollable pane on screen; pick one, then scroll with J/K or the arrows, page with Space, and jump to the top or bottom with T and B.
- **Jump grid (E):** a 3×3 grid labelled Q W E / A S D / Z X C covers the screen and zooms in with each press, so two or three keys reach any point. Works in every app.

## Learn it in two minutes

A guided setup ends with a hands-on try, the Practice window has nine short lessons that tick themselves off, and H shows every key whenever you forget one.

## Private

Keystrokes are never stored or sent. The app only goes online to activate and re-check your license and to check for updates. It needs Accessibility permission (Screen Recording only if you turn on reading on-screen text).
