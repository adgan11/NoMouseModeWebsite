Mouse Keys is the free mouse that's already in your Mac. **It's perfect for a dead trackpad, but you can't type while it's on and it has no right-click key**, which is why people who use it daily look for an alternative.

> **Short answer:** Mouse Keys is a free, built-in macOS feature: turn it on in System Settings > Accessibility > Pointer Control > Mouse Keys, then move the pointer with `7` `8` `9` `U` `O` `J` `K` `L` and click with `I`. It works with no mouse or trackpad at all, so it's the right first step when one breaks. Its limits show up with daily use: you can't type while it's on, its page lists no right-click key, and it can't click a button by name. ## How to turn on Mouse Keys on a Mac

1. Choose **Apple menu > System Settings**, then click **Accessibility**.
2. Click **Pointer Control** and turn on **Mouse Keys**.
3. Click the **info button** next to Mouse Keys and turn on **Press the Option key five times to toggle Mouse Keys**. Now you can switch it on and off without opening Settings.
4. In the same options, set **Initial delay** and **Maximum speed** to taste.

No working pointer at all? Press `Option-Command-F5` (or triple-press Touch ID) to open the Accessibility Shortcuts panel, which can turn Mouse Keys on or off. The path above is the same in Apple's guides for macOS Sequoia 15 and Tahoe 26.

### The Mouse Keys keys

| Action | Keyboard (no keypad) | Numeric keypad |
|---|---|---|
| Move the pointer | 7 8 9 U O J K L | 7 8 9 4 6 1 2 3 |
| Click | I | 5 |
| Press and hold (start a drag) | M | 0 |
| Release | . | . |

The movement keys sit in a ring around the click key (`I`, or keypad `5`), laid out like the numeric keypad.

## Mouse Keys not working? Check these first

- **"My keyboard stopped typing."** That's Mouse Keys doing its job. Apple says: "When Mouse Keys is on, you can't use the keyboard or numeric keypad to enter text." Turn it off to type.
- **It turned itself off (or on).** If the Option-five-times option is set, pressing `Option` repeatedly while typing can toggle it by accident. Turn it back on the same way.
- **The arrow keys do nothing.** Mouse Keys doesn't use them. On a laptop, use `7` `8` `9` `U` `O` `J` `K` `L`.
- **The trackpad stopped working.** Check whether **Ignore built-in trackpad when Mouse Keys is on** is turned on in the Mouse Keys options.
- **The pointer is painfully slow.** Raise **Maximum speed** and lower **Initial delay**.

If the real problem is a mouse or trackpad that stopped responding, our [Mac trackpad not working](https://nomousemode.com/mac-trackpad-not-working) guide walks through Apple's fixes, including turning Bluetooth on without a pointer.

## Where Mouse Keys falls short

Mouse Keys is a good emergency tool and fine for the odd click. As a daily driver, its limits add up:

- **You can't type while it's on.** Every time you want to fill in a field, you toggle it off, type, and toggle it on again.
- **No right-click key.** Apple's Mouse Keys page lists none. For right-clicks you turn on Alternate pointer actions in the same settings pane, or use Voice Control. See [how to right-click without a mouse](https://nomousemode.com/right-click-without-mouse-mac).
- **Fixed keys.** The options Apple lists are the Option toggle, ignoring the trackpad, initial delay and maximum speed. There's no setting to choose different movement keys.
- **No scrolling keys, middle click or back/forward** on Apple's page.
- **Aiming is slow.** To press a button across the screen, you steer the pointer all the way there. There are no labels and no grid.

macOS has other built-ins that help: [Full Keyboard Access](https://nomousemode.com/full-keyboard-access-mac) moves between controls with Tab, and Voice Control can label buttons by voice. Each fills one gap, but none is a complete keyboard mouse.

## FAQ

### How do I turn on Mouse Keys on a Mac?

Choose Apple menu > System Settings > Accessibility > Pointer Control, then turn on Mouse Keys. To toggle it from the keyboard later, click the info button next to Mouse Keys and turn on "Press the Option key five times to toggle Mouse Keys". You can also press Option-Command-F5 to open the Accessibility Shortcuts panel.

### Which keys move the pointer with Mouse Keys?

On a keyboard without a numeric keypad: 7 8 9 U O J K L move the pointer, I clicks, M presses and holds the button, and the period (.) releases it. With a keypad: 7 8 9 4 6 1 2 3 move, 5 clicks, 0 holds and . releases.

### Why can't I type after turning on Mouse Keys?

That's how Mouse Keys works. Apple says that when Mouse Keys is on, you can't use the keyboard or numeric keypad to enter text. Turn it off (press Option five times if you turned that option on) to type again.

### How do I right-click with Mouse Keys?

Apple's Mouse Keys page lists no right-click key. For a right-click from the keyboard, turn on Alternate pointer actions in the same Pointer Control settings, or use Voice Control. [No Mouse Mode](https://nomousemode.com/) right-clicks with Return. More options in [how to right-click without a mouse](https://nomousemode.com/right-click-without-mouse-mac).

### Why is Mouse Keys not working on my Mac?

Check that it's actually on (pressing Option five times toggles it if that option is set, so it's easy to switch off by accident). On a laptop, use 7 8 9 U O J K L, not the arrow keys. If the trackpad stopped responding instead, look for "Ignore built-in trackpad when Mouse Keys is on" in its options. If the pointer feels slow, change Initial delay and Maximum speed there.
