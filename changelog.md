# No Mouse Mode changelog

Release notes for every version of No Mouse Mode, newest first. HTML version: https://nomousemode.com/changelog

## 2.0.1 (2026-11-12)

- Label size: make click labels and scroll-area numbers bigger or smaller in Settings → Clicking (Small, Default, Large or Extra Large), so they're easy to read on a laptop screen.

## 2.0.0 (2026-10-22)

- Click labels, anywhere: press ⇧⌘Space in any app (No Mouse Mode on or off) and type the letters on any button, link, tab, menu, menu bar item or Dock icon. Hold ⇧ to right-click, ⌘ to ⌘-click, ⌥ to double-click or ⌃ to just move there. Tab keeps the labels open for several clicks. Labels for a window stay the same each time.
- Type to click: press ⇧⌘/ (or / while it's on), type part of a button's or text's name, press Return.
- Scroll areas: press ⇧⌘J (or J) to number every scrollable pane, then scroll, page with Space, or jump to the top or bottom with T and B.
- Labels in every app (optional): reads on-screen text with Apple's on-device text recognition for apps that don't describe their buttons. Needs Screen Recording; nothing is saved or sent.
- Middle click (M) and the back and forward buttons ([ and ]).
- Saved spots: ⇧1–9 saves where the pointer is, 1–9 jumps back. ` jumps back to where the pointer was before its last jump. Tab jumps to the next display.
- Turns itself off in apps you choose (Settings → Apps, or from the menu bar), and when you click into a text box so you can type.
- Easy to learn: a guided setup with a hands-on try, a Practice window with nine lessons, H shows every key, and key tips on the labels and grid.
- Jump grid: press E and pick a cell with Q W E / A S D / Z X C to land anywhere on screen in two or three key presses.
- Smoother movement that keeps pace with your display (120 Hz on ProMotion), starts gently so a tap nudges, and reaches full speed in a fraction of a second. Hold Q to move exactly one point per tap.
- Smooth scrolling, now sideways too (← →). Shift scrolls faster instead of sideways.
- Hold to use: optionally hold Right Command or Right Option to use No Mouse Mode only while the key is down.
- Every key and shortcut can be changed, with one button to put them all back. Label colors: yellow, black or white.
- Open at login, redesigned Settings, a new black-and-white look, and automatic updates.
- License keys: activate in Settings → License; licenses for 1, 3, 5 or 20 Macs. Version 1 customers keep No Mouse Mode working on the Mac they already use, with no key needed.

## 1.0.1 (2026-10-18)

- Runs on macOS 14 Sonoma and newer (1.0 needed the newest macOS).
- Shortcuts with Command, Control or Option (Cmd+S, Cmd+W…) reach your apps while No Mouse Mode is on.
- Double- and triple-click work.
- Shift boost reacts instantly.
- The on/off badge appears on the display you're using.
